Privacy Policy for Learn Hindi Global

Effective Date: November 2026

NITVID Enterprises ("we", "our", or "us") built the Learn Hindi Global app as a commercial educational application. This page informs visitors regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information.

1. Information We Collect

When you create a student account, we store your name and email address locally on your device to personalize your learning experience and track lesson progress. We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties.

2. Microphone and Audio Permissions

Our app requests microphone permission solely for interactive Hindi pronunciation practice. Voice inputs are processed locally on your device and are never recorded, stored, or transmitted to external servers.

3. In-App Purchases

All payments and subscriptions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect or store your credit card, UPI, or banking details.

4. Children's Privacy

Learn Hindi Global is designed to be safe for learners of all ages, including children under 13, and complies with Google Play Families policies.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact NITVID Enterprises via our Google Play Store developer contact details.
